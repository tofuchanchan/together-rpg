Exactly five enemies on the right half: two large mushroom brutes and three small forest goblins. Allies visually distinct from enemies. Clearly grounded feet and cast shadows. Dynamic motion, clean weapon silhouette. Limited sharp fire and arrow effects, one amber sword-swing trail, one flat red outlined enemy attack warning circle, readable contact. No cinematic blur.

Top center small room indicator exactly "03 / 06". Top right small minimap and coin icon with "128". Bottom left a compact warrior portrait and health readout "120 / 120". Bottom center exactly TWO large square skill icons: shield charge labeled "Q" and whirlwind labeled "E", followed by a smaller dodge icon labeled "SPACE". Small noninteractive text "AUTO" adjacent to the portrait signifies automatic basic attacks; no separate manual basic attack button. Thin experience bar across bottom with text "LV. 4". Strong squash-and-stretch implication in the warrior pose. Moss green, warm cream stone, burnt orange mushrooms, teal hero accents, purple mage.
