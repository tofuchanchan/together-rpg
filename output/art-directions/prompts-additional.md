STYLE OVERRIDE is the most important requirement: visual direction strongly inspired by Brotato's minimal absurd cartoon art, with ORIGINAL medieval fantasy characters. Flat vector-like digital drawing, very thick uniform black outlines, very simple bean-shaped bodies, tiny stub feet, dot eyes or fierce eyebrow eyes, floating mitten hands, oversized readable equipment; warrior is a short oval bean knight with shield and sword, mage a round bean wearing enormous purple wizard hat, archer a short green-hooded bean with bow. Heroes squat, around 1.5 heads tall. Mushroom enemies round bulbous thick-outline blobs, goblins simple green bean silhouettes. Absolutely NOT detailed anatomy, NOT painterly, NOT 3D, NOT gradients or realistic texture.
SIMPLIFY the shared forest ruin scene radically: huge quiet desaturated sage floor occupying 75 percent of view, just scattered simple stone slabs; perimeter trees are a few simple dark green bubble silhouettes, ruined arch rendered in chunky flat shapes. Keep 45 degree overhead arena depth, do NOT become a side-scrolling game. Heroes and enemies sparse, large clear flat color patches, restrained two-tone shading only. A round flat fireball with jagged yellow tail, thick cream slash crescent, simple arrow, precise red warning ring. Strong graphic contrast. UI also simple flat charcoal rounded panels, thick black borders, bright bars and plain icons, few embellishments, not ornate gold. This direction must be immediately identifiable as radically more minimal and funnier than a hand-painted fantasy game. No potato franchise characters, draw original fantasy bean adventurers.
