References: output/coop-design/D-coop-combat.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent PNG sprite sheet for an actual 2D co-op action roguelite.
The supplied forest gameplay illustration is STYLE AND CHARACTER IDENTITY REFERENCE ONLY. Recreate its illustrated chubby characters faithfully, not the environment or UI. Broad friendly bean silhouettes, oversized head, tiny feet, very thick near-black clean outlines, flat richly colored cel shading with one soft shaded plane and tasteful hand-painted finish. Same 45-degree elevated oblique game camera in every cell. Absolutely not 3D, pixel art, geometric stick figures or thin-line vector placeholders.
OUTPUT: 2048 x 2048 square RGBA, genuinely TRANSPARENT background, no checkerboard, white backdrop, ground shadow, grid lines, labels, lettering, effects, UI or scenery.
STRICT GRID: exactly 4 columns and 8 rows, 32 separate full-body sprites. Each invisible cell is 512 wide by 256 high. Sprite foot anchor at the horizontal center of its cell and 220px down from cell top; body (not weapon/cape) remains centered consistently. Head-to-foot height approximately 190px, identical scale across all cells. Keep every character/equipment wholly inside its cell with empty margins; no cropping. Do not let rows/columns touch.
Direction is FIXED per row. Row 1: SOUTH / front facing camera. Row 2: SOUTHWEST / front-left three-quarter. Row 3: WEST / left profile. Row 4: NORTHWEST / back-left three-quarter. Row 5: NORTH / true rear view, no face visible. Row 6: NORTHEAST / back-right three-quarter. Row 7: EAST / right profile. Row 8: SOUTHEAST / front-right three-quarter. Eight separately drawn genuine views, never pretend a flipped front is a rear.
Each row shows a four-pose locomotion sequence. Column 1: neutral relaxed standing, feet under body. Column 2: running contact pose, anatomical left boot ahead/right boot behind, small forward lean. Column 3: running passing pose, knees passing under body, slight lift, cape/cloth follows motion. Column 4: running opposite contact, right boot ahead/left boot behind. The directional torso/head shape, sizes, outfit and handedness must remain identical across each row; only pose/legs/cloth change. Do NOT repeat the same standing pose 4 times. No movement across cell; animate in place.

Subject: the exact adorable small knight from the reference: squat oval silver steel helmet with a domed smooth top (NO plume/crest), three bold vertical black visor slits, a small off-white lower rim, rounded teal tunic and simple silver shoulder armor, tan belt, short dark boots, bright muted red flowing cape knotted at neck. Right anatomical hand holds short broad silver sword with dark hilt; left anatomical hand holds a teal heater shield with silver border and a simple cream sprout/bird emblem. Readable black outline around every major shape. Friendly compact proportions, approx two heads tall, not a long torso, no realistic anatomy or ornate equipment. Keep anatomical sword/shield sides correct when seen from behind.
```
